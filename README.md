ALY 6980 Capstone Course

During my ALY 6980 capstone, I worked with an industry sponsor through an XN partnership to address a real business challenge requiring data‑driven decision support. Our task was to design an analytical solution that would help the sponsor understand performance patterns and make more informed strategic and tactical decisions.

To accomplish this, my team built an end‑to‑end analytical tool that featured an interactive dashboard supported by exploratory data analysis, stakeholder interviews, and iterative refinement. I contributed to the modeling, data preparation, and insight development, and helped ensure the dashboard was intuitive, actionable, and aligned with the sponsor’s operational needs. We delivered the final solution in a professional presentation that demonstrated how the tool could guide decision‑making and improve visibility into key performance drivers.

In parallel, I developed an individual capstone proposal that expanded the project’s scope by analyzing how digital tools like CRM systems, predictive analytics, and data governance frameworks can strengthen franchise operations. After evaluating leadership considerations, ethical implications, and governance challenges, I designed a multi‑phase implementation plan supported by data collection strategies, benchmarking, and predictive modeling.

Together, these projects strengthened my ability to translate complex data into strategic recommendations, build functional analytical tools, and communicate insights clearly to real stakeholders. They demonstrated our capacity to integrate technical skills with organizational strategy and deliver analytics solutions that support long‑term operational success.
